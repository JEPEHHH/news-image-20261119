## Lead — ICJ finds Myanmar committed genocide

- Reuters, world news summary, 19 Nov 2026: "World court finds Myanmar
  committed genocide against the Rohingya population … the first time a state
  has been found responsible for genocide at the court."
  https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4009516-reuters-world-news-summary
- Democracy Now!, headlines for 19 Nov 2026: the ruling follows the 2017

  https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/19/headlines
